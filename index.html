Ufanele means “you are worthy” in Ndebele.

The magazine

Faith, lifestyle and empowerment, together

Across Africa, women are navigating identity, faith and modern culture in a rapidly changing world. Glossy magazines rarely speak to her faith. Faith publications rarely speak her language.

Ufanele sits in the space between, affirming her worth, inspiring excellence and celebrating her identity in Christ.

  • Victorious testimonies
  • Motivational features
  • Vibrant fashion and entertainment
  • Authentic African representation

The creators

Meet the people behind Ufanele

Why this magazine exists.

Kundai Mazarura

Editor

“Together, let us blossom in worth.”

In the 1st Edition’s Editor’s Note, Kundai writes that Ufanele “was born from a simple yet profound truth: you are worthy.” Her hope is that every page helps women of faith embrace their uniqueness, walk boldly in their calling, and know they are worthy of love, of joy and of confidence.

Rutendo Moyo

Co-founder and Creative Director

“I wanted a magazine that looks like us.”

Rutendo leads the look and feel of every page. She started Ufanele after years of searching shelves for a magazine that held faith and style in the same hand, and deciding to build it herself so that every woman could see herself in it.

Nokuthula Ncube

Co-founder and Head of Content

“Every woman has a story worth telling.”

Nokuthula shapes the testimonies, features and stories. She joined Ufanele because she believes women grow when they hear honest stories of faith, healing and everyday courage, and she wanted a place where those stories could be printed, kept and passed on.
